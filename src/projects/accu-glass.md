## Context

Accu-Glass Products manufactures components used in high and ultra-high vacuum systems. The internship required strict adherence to tolerances and quality standards while supporting real production work.

## CNC production

I operated Haas 3-axis CNC mills and 2- and 4-axis lathes for production runs used in capital projects. Working with senior machinists and engineers, I helped troubleshoot manufacturing issues and improve machining efficiency while protecting part quality.

## CAD documentation and quality

Beyond the machines, I created SolidWorks models and technical drawings for manufacturing and QA clarity. I collaborated with machinists on work-holding solutions and inspected parts with calipers, micrometers, gauge pins, and leak-testing equipment.

## Why it matters

This role is a differentiator for mechanical design candidates: direct time on Haas equipment, drawing ownership, and quality accountability for precision hardware.
