## Context

At Parker Meggitt I supported manufacturing engineering for fire-suppression related production. The work mixed fixture design, ergonomics/safety improvements, failure analysis support, and production documentation.

## Manufacturing engineering contributions

- Designed custom fixtures to improve process safety and productivity
- Participated in failure analyses and corrective-action development with design engineers
- Updated and clarified work instructions for production personnel
- Applied Lean-aligned thinking to reduce friction in manufacturing processes

## Pressure gauge test fixture

A key project was a pressure-gauge testing fixture that allowed simultaneous cycling of multiple gauges. I created detailed CAD models and assembly drawings, engineered a protective safety shield for personnel, and worked with Tool Design Engineers to validate the design and move it toward fabrication.

This internship is evidence of manufacturing engineering judgment: designing tooling that protects people, shortens repetitive test work, and can be built by a toolroom — not only modeled in CAD.
