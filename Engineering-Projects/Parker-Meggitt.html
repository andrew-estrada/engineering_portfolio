Parker Meggitt

Manufacturing Engineering Internship

At Parker-Meggitt, I contributed to enhancing production efficiency, safety, and ergonomics through engineering solutions. I designed custom fixtures that improved process safety and increased productivity while aligning with Lean manufacturing principles. Collaborating closely with design engineers, I participated in failure analyses and developed effective corrective actions to optimize manufacturing processes. Additionally, I supported production personnel by updating and clarifying work instructions, ensuring smooth operations and consistent product quality.

Pressure Gauge Fixture

One of my key projects involved the development of a pressure gauge testing fixture that enabled simultaneous cycling of multiple gauges. I created detailed CAD models and assembly drawings, engineered a protective safety shield for personnel, and worked with Tool Design Engineers to validate the fixture’s design and facilitate its fabrication. This project showcased my ability to merge safety, innovation, and efficiency in a fast-paced manufacturing environment.
